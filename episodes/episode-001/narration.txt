ONLY ONE GOALKEEPER HAS EVER WON THE BALLON D'OR. NOT BUFFON. NOT NEUER. NOT CASILLAS. HIS NAME WAS LEV YASHIN. IN 1963, HE ACHIEVED SOMETHING NO OTHER GOALKEEPER HAS EVER MATCHED. THEY CALLED HIM THE BLACK SPIDER. WITH LIGHTNING-FAST REFLEXES AND FEARLESS CONTROL OF HIS PENALTY AREA, YASHIN CHANGED THE WAY GOALKEEPERS PLAYED. MORE THAN SIXTY YEARS LATER, HIS RECORD STILL STANDS. WHO'S NEXT?
